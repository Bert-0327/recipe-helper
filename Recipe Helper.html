    Easy fill in
    
        
    Paste your recipe text to fill in the form. Review and edit it, then click Save recipe. This simple helper recognizes headings, amounts, and numbered steps. It does not use AI or guess missing details.
    
        
    Best format: English recipe title, yield, an Ingredients section with one ingredient per line, then an Instructions section. For example: 2 cups cooked rice.
    
        
    
          Formatting guide and example
          
      
            
    • Put the recipe name on the first line. Before the sections, add a yield such as Serves: 2, Makes: 8 pieces, or Yield: 8 pieces.
      • 
            
    • Use an Ingredients or What you'll need heading. Put each ingredient on its own line, with the amount, unit, then name: 2 cups cooked rice. Common units include cups, tbsp, tsp, g, ml, cans, and sheets. Decimals and fractions such as 0.5, 1/2, and 1 1/2 work.
      • 
            
    • Use Instructions, Directions, Method, Steps, or Preparation for cooking steps. Numbered steps are welcome.
      • 
            
    • Amounts such as 1–2, a little, or to taste need manual review. Keep them in the text for reference; the helper cannot turn them into an exact amount. Check unfamiliar units and package sizes yourself.
      • 
            
    • Paste recipe text, not a video or link. This helper does not watch videos, open links, or translate. Use up to 30,000 characters.
      • 
            
    • Check the recipe name, ingredient names, quantities, units, yield, and steps in the filled form. Missing amounts and yield stay blank and are flagged; fill them before saving. Your original text stays available. Nothing saves until you click Save recipe.
      • 
          
    
          
    A short example — you can copy this format:
    
          
    Simple rice bowl
Serves: 2

Ingredients
2 cups cooked rice
1 tbsp soy sauce

Instructions
1. Heat the cooked rice until hot.
2. Stir in the soy sauce and serve.
