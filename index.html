Build your kitchen confidence

Three habits for better cooking.

01 /

Prep before you cook

Read the whole recipe first. Gather your ingredients, measure what you need, and chop vegetables before turning on the heat. Use a stable cutting board and handle sharp knives carefully.

02 /

Get to know your heat

A simmer has gentle bubbles; a boil bubbles vigorously. Sauté in a little oil over medium to medium-high heat, watching the pan. Give ingredients room so they brown evenly.

03 /

Measure, then adjust

Use level spoonfuls and consistent units. For baking, weigh ingredients when possible. In savory cooking, add seasoning gradually and taste when the food is safe to eat.

Your starting kit: a chef's knife, cutting board, saucepan, frying pan, mixing bowl, measuring spoons, and a kitchen scale.

How do I keep my recipes safe?

Recipes save in this browser on this device. Use Export backup regularly to download a JSON copy. Import backup adds recipes from a saved backup and keeps existing recipes. Export before moving the page or switching browsers, since saved recipes may not follow you.

Does scaling change cooking time?

Ingredient amounts scale proportionally; cooking time and oven temperature do not change automatically. Choose suitable cookware and check doneness. For a fraction of an egg, beat it first and weigh the needed portion. The helper does not convert between measurement units.
